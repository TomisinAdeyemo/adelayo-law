Family-Based Immigration

Helping families understand the process of building a life together.

U.S. immigration law provides family-based pathways for certain relatives of U.S. citizens and lawful permanent residents. The correct category depends on the relationship, the petitioner’s status and the applicant’s circumstances. Adelayo Law assists families in understanding the available routes and the process involved.

Family-based immigration is about more than forms. It is about the people waiting to build a life together.

01

Immediate relatives

The immediate-relative category includes certain close relatives of U.S. citizens. Examples described by the firm include a spouse of a U.S. citizen, an unmarried child under 21 of a U.S. citizen, and a parent of a U.S. citizen who is at least 21 years old.

02

Family preference categories

Other qualifying family relationships fall within preference categories. Examples include unmarried sons and daughters of U.S. citizens; spouses and certain children of permanent residents; married sons and daughters of U.S. citizens and their qualifying family members; and brothers and sisters of U.S. citizens who are at least 21 years old. Availability and waiting periods depend on the category and individual circumstances.

03

Green cards through a family member

A qualifying family relationship may provide a basis to seek an immigrant visa or lawful permanent residence. The process depends on the petitioner’s immigration status, the relationship and whether the applicant completes processing inside or outside the United States.

04

Consular processing

For applicants completing immigrant visa processing abroad, consular processing involves preparing the required petition and supporting documents, then proceeding through the relevant visa-processing steps. We help clients understand the sequence and prepare for the process.

05

Adjustment of status

Some eligible applicants who are already in the United States may apply to become lawful permanent residents without travelling abroad for an immigrant visa. The source material describes inspection and admission or parole, along with meeting the requirements of a qualifying green-card category, as important eligibility considerations.

06

Fiancé(e) visas

The K-1 visa is a route for the foreign-citizen fiancé(e) of a U.S. citizen to come to the United States to marry within the required period. The process has specific requirements and can change over time, making careful case preparation important.

07

Marriage equality & immigration

The firm’s immigration practice information notes that qualifying same-sex marriages are recognised for federal immigration purposes. U.S. citizens and lawful permanent residents may petition for eligible foreign-born spouses, subject to the applicable immigration requirements.
