01

A practice built around people

Immigration law can involve complicated requirements, important deadlines and decisions that affect an entire family. We begin by understanding your circumstances, then help you assess the options that may be available and the steps involved.

02

Asylum law

People who have experienced persecution, or who have a well-founded fear of persecution, may be eligible to seek asylum in the United States. The legal definition considers persecution connected to race, religion, nationality, membership in a particular social group or political opinion. Adelayo Law represents people from different countries who are seeking protection.

03

Citizenship & naturalization

Some people may acquire citizenship through a parent; others may qualify through naturalization. We can help review your circumstances, consider whether you may be eligible and guide you through the application process.

04

Deportation & removal defense

Non-citizens may face removal proceedings in a range of circumstances, including certain criminal histories, prior removal orders, visa overstays or entry without inspection. Adelayo Law represents individuals seeking to remain in the United States and can help evaluate the issues affecting a removal matter.

05

Green cards & adjustment of status

For some eligible applicants already in the United States, adjustment of status may provide a route to lawful permanent residence without completing immigrant visa processing abroad. Eligibility depends on the applicant’s circumstances and the immigration category involved.

06

Consular processing

When an immigrant visa must be processed through a U.S. consulate abroad, the process involves several stages and supporting documents. We assist clients in understanding the process and preparing for the steps ahead.

07

Fiancé(e) visas — K-1

The K-1 fiancé(e) visa may allow the foreign-citizen fiancé(e) of a U.S. citizen to travel to the United States to marry within the required period. The process has specific requirements and can change; careful preparation helps keep the application organised.

08

Marriage & immigration

U.S. immigration law recognises qualifying same-sex marriages for federal immigration purposes. U.S. citizens and lawful permanent residents may be able to petition for eligible foreign-born spouses, subject to the applicable requirements.
