01

Misdemeanors & felonies

Adelayo Law represents people facing a range of misdemeanor and felony allegations in Arizona. The nature of the charge, the available evidence and the circumstances of the case all matter when considering a defence strategy.

02

Alcohol-related offenses & DUI

Alcohol-related allegations can include DUI and matters involving underage possession or consumption. The facts surrounding an investigation, stop, testing and arrest may be important to evaluating a case.

03

Traffic offenses

We assist with criminal traffic matters, including driving on a suspended license, speeding and criminal speeding allegations.

04

Theft offenses

Theft-related charges may include shoplifting, burglary, robbery and theft of a means of transportation. The specific allegation and evidence can affect the potential consequences and available responses.

05

Drug possession

Arizona drug cases may involve allegations concerning dangerous drugs, narcotics, prescription medication or other controlled substances. The substance, amount and surrounding circumstances can affect how a charge is classified.

06

Weapons charges

Arizona weapons allegations can include misconduct involving weapons, defaced firearms and other conduct regulated by state law. The circumstances and statutory provision involved are central to understanding the charge.

07

Offenses involving allegations of harm

The firm’s criminal defence practice includes allegations such as assault, aggravated assault, domestic violence, homicide and manslaughter, as well as other serious offenses against people.

08

Sex offenses & hate crimes

The firm also identifies sexual-offense allegations and hate crimes among the matters it handles. These cases can be especially serious and require careful attention to the allegations and applicable law.

09

Before charges are filed

An investigation may be underway even if no citation or formal charge has been issued. If police are seeking to question you or contact you about allegations, early legal advice may be important. The pre-charge stage can be a critical time to protect your interests.

10

Setting aside a conviction

Arizona law may allow some people who have completed the applicable sentence or probation requirements to ask a court to set aside a judgment of guilt under A.R.S. § 13-905. Eligibility and effects depend on the individual case; this is not the same as erasing every record of the conviction.

The information on this page is general information about the firm’s practice areas and is not legal advice. Immigration and criminal laws can change, and eligibility or outcomes depend on the facts of each matter. Speak with an attorney about your specific circumstances.
