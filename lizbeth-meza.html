01CLIENT SUPPORT

Supporting Families Through the Wait

Born and raised in Phoenix, Arizona, Lizbeth Meza is a first-generation college student who brings both heart and creativity to Adelayo Law Firm.

When you call Adelayo Law Firm, Lizbeth is usually the first voice you'll hear. She handles client intake: she listens to each caller's situation, gathers the information the legal team needs, and schedules consultations, so every new client starts off feeling welcomed, heard, and in good hands.

As a Legal Assistant, Lizbeth works alongside the firm's attorney and paralegals on:

  • DACA requests
  • Naturalization applications (Form N-400)
  • Adjustment of status applications (Form I-485)

Lizbeth knows that waiting for an immigration decision can be one of the most stressful times in a family's life. She makes sure clients feel supported and informed throughout the process, and one of the most rewarding parts of her job is sharing in their relief and joy when the approval finally arrives.

02CREATIVE WORK

The Creative Voice of the Firm

As the firm's Social Media Manager, Lizbeth brings Adelayo Law Firm's story to life online. She brainstorms ideas, designs graphics, and creates content that makes complicated immigration topics easier to understand. She also uses the firm's platforms to celebrate client milestones, with their permission, and to introduce followers to the people behind the firm.

She is pursuing her education in Digital Marketing and Social Media, building on the creative work she already loves.
