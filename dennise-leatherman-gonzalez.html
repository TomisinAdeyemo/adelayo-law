01HER STORY

Rooted in the Border Community

Dennise Leatherman Gonzalez was born and raised in Douglas, Arizona, a border town where she grew up seeing how much good legal help matters to families. Her Mexican heritage and fluency in Spanish give her a deep connection to the Latino community. That connection drives her passion for helping individuals and families find their way through the U.S. legal system, especially in immigration and criminal matters.

02EXPERIENCE

Experience You Can Count On

Dennise earned her Bachelor of Arts in Legal Studies from Dominican University in River Forest, Illinois, and has worked in the legal field since 2012. As the firm's longest-serving team member, she brings more than a decade of experience and deep institutional knowledge to every case.

Over her career, she has supported matters in a wide range of areas, including:

  • Immigration Law
  • Criminal Defense and DUI
  • Personal Injury
  • Estate Planning
  • Family Law
03CLIENT CARE

Dedicated to Every Client

Dennise is often the first person clients speak with, and she makes sure they feel heard from the start. She provides compassionate, attentive support at every stage of a case, and she believes every person deserves to be treated with dignity, respect, and understanding, especially during the most difficult moments of their lives.

04BEYOND THE OFFICE

Beyond the Office

Outside the office, Dennise enjoys spending time with her family and traveling.
