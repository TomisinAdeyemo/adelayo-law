Adelayo Law focuses on U.S. immigration and criminal defense, representing individuals and families through decisions that can shape their lives and futures. Whether you are seeking a path to lawful status, working toward citizenship, facing removal proceedings or responding to criminal charges, our team provides thoughtful guidance and committed representation at every step.

BAMIDELE ‘BAMI’ ADELAYO

Bamidele “Bami” Adelayo represents immigrants and people facing criminal charges, bringing focused experience and a personal commitment to each client’s case.

Bami is licensed to practice law in Arizona, Illinois, New Jersey and Minnesota. He grew up in the southwestern part of Nigeria and speaks fluent Yoruba.

He earned his LLB from Olabisi Onabanjo University, formerly Ogun State University, and obtained both his Juris Doctor and LLM, with an emphasis on international law, from Washington University School of Law in St. Louis, Missouri.
