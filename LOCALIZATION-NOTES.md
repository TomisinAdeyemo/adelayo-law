# Adelayo Law manual localization

The custom language selector is connected to `translations.js` and supports English, Yorùbá, Igbo and Hausa. The selected language is saved in local storage and follows visitors as they move between the site's HTML pages.

## Current coverage
This initial manual localization release translates the shared navigation, primary homepage messaging, selected section headings and calls to action, and selected practice-page headings. Any detailed legal explanation or page copy not present in the dictionaries remains in English. A notice appears when a non-English language is selected so visitors are not led to believe that every legal detail has been translated.

## Before publication
Yorùbá, Igbo and Hausa strings are draft translations and should be reviewed by fluent speakers and an Arizona-licensed attorney familiar with the relevant legal terminology. Do not rely on these translations as legal advice. Add approved translations to the corresponding language dictionary in `translations.js` as the remaining page copy is reviewed.
