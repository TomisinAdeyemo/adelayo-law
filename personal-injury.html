01

A focused approach to your claim

The legal process after an injury can feel complicated while you are trying to recover. Our team works to understand what happened, explain the process and pursue the compensation that may be available under the circumstances.

02

Car accidents

A collision can lead to medical expenses, missed work and lasting physical effects. Getting medical attention, preserving information about the incident and understanding communications from insurers can all be important after a crash.

03

Motorcycle accidents

Motorcycle collisions can result in serious injuries. A careful review of the incident, available records and the impact on the injured person is important when evaluating a claim.

04

Pedestrian accidents

People on foot are vulnerable when struck by a vehicle. If you or a loved one has been injured as a pedestrian, the circumstances of the collision and resulting harm should be carefully documented.

05

Premises liability

Some injury claims arise from dangerous conditions on property. The nature of the hazard, who controlled the property and what was known about the condition may all be relevant.

06

Catastrophic injury & traumatic brain injury

Serious injuries can affect a person’s independence, ability to work and long-term care needs. The firm’s personal injury practice information identifies catastrophic injuries and traumatic brain injuries among the matters it handles.

07

Wrongful death

When a person dies because of another party’s negligence, surviving family members may have questions about a wrongful death claim. Adelayo Law describes pursuing compensation and justice for families affected by such a loss.

08

After a car accident

If you are able, check whether anyone needs medical attention and call emergency services when necessary. Contact law enforcement and obtain the police report information. Collect the other driver’s contact and insurance details, and the names and contact information of witnesses. Avoid discussing fault at the scene.

09

In the days that follow

Seek medical attention promptly and keep records relating to your injuries and treatment. Before signing documents or discussing the claim with the other party’s insurance company, consider speaking with an attorney about your options.
