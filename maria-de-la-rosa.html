01HER STORY

A Personal Understanding of the Immigrant Journey

Born in Sonora, Mexico, and raised in Arizona, Maria De la Rosa knows the immigrant experience firsthand. Growing up between two cultures and two countries gave her a deep appreciation for the challenges, uncertainty, and sacrifices that come with building a life in a new place.

She brings that perspective to every client she works with at Adelayo Law Firm. Maria knows that behind every immigration case is a person or a family with a unique story, and she makes sure each client feels heard, understood, and respected. She approaches every case with compassion, careful attention to detail, and a commitment to presenting each client's story accurately and completely.

02LEGAL SUPPORT

Removal Defense and Humanitarian Relief

Maria works primarily with clients in removal proceedings and those seeking humanitarian protection, including:

  • Asylum
  • Violence Against Women Act (VAWA) self-petitions
  • U visas for victims of crime

Under the supervision of the firm's attorney, she communicates with clients, prepares cases, and gathers and organizes the supporting evidence each application needs, so that every client's case is presented as fully and as strongly as their circumstances allow.

03EDUCATION & TECHNOLOGY

Education and Firm Technology

Maria earned a Bachelor's degree in Government with an emphasis in Legal Studies and a Master of Business Administration (MBA), both from Grand Canyon University.

She puts that business training to work helping oversee the firm's technology, including its software systems, AI tools, and ongoing development projects, so that the firm keeps pace with the tools that help it serve clients better. She also works with the firm's vendors and service providers on the firm's behalf.

04OUTSIDE THE OFFICE

Beyond the Office

Outside the office, Maria enjoys spending time with her husband and sons, working out at the gym, and learning new languages, currently French. She also loves to read, though with toddlers at home, she manages about one page a day.
