01THE JOURNEY

I Know This Journey Because I Have Made It

In 1997, Bamidele Adelayo left Nigeria for the United States with a law degree, a suitcase, and the determination to build a new life far from home. He knows what it feels like to arrive in a new country, face a complicated legal system, and worry about the future of your family.

Today, as founder and principal attorney of Adelayo Law Firm, PLLC, in Phoenix, Arizona, he brings that understanding to every client he represents. Since 2010, the firm has helped individuals and families with immigration matters, criminal charges, and personal injury claims. Bami gives every case personal attention, honest advice, and hard work.

02TRIAL EXPERIENCE

Experience on Both Sides of the Courtroom

Few defense attorneys have seen the criminal justice system from every angle. Bami has.

From 2014 to 2024, Bami served as a Contract Attorney with the City of Phoenix Public Defender’s Office, defending clients charged with misdemeanors in Phoenix Municipal Court. That decade of local practice gave him a deep working knowledge of Phoenix’s courts, procedures, and prosecutors.

Earlier in his career, as an Assistant Deputy Public Defender with the New Jersey Office of the Public Defender, he represented clients from arraignment through trial. He litigated motions to suppress evidence, negotiated pleas, and tried cases before juries.

As an Assistant State’s Attorney with the Will County State’s Attorney’s Office in Illinois, he prosecuted criminal and traffic cases and tried more than 40 jury and bench trials. He was later promoted to the office’s Complaint Office, where he worked with senior prosecutors and law enforcement to review and file felony charges, drafted search warrants, and presented cases to the grand jury.

That experience gives his clients a real advantage. He knows how prosecutors build their cases, where those cases are weak, and how to challenge them.

03WHAT HE DOES

Practice Areas

Immigration Law

Bami represents individuals and families before U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), the U.S. Immigration Courts, and the Board of Immigration Appeals. His practice includes family-based petitions, adjustment of status, and removal defense. He also advises non-citizens on the immigration consequences of criminal charges, an area where a mistake can cost someone their right to remain in the United States.

Criminal Defense

Bami defends clients charged with felonies and misdemeanors in Arizona courts. His cases include DUI, drug offenses, theft, fraud, assault, domestic violence, and weapons charges. He handles each case from the first appearance through motions, negotiation, and trial.

Personal Injury

Bami accepts a select number of personal injury cases, representing people injured in car accidents and pedestrian accidents. Taking on only a limited number of these cases lets him give each one the personal attention it deserves as he works to recover compensation for medical bills, lost wages, and pain and suffering.
